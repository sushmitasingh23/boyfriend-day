Happy Boyfriend's Day, Toshu. 🥹❤️

I don't know if I say it enough, but I'm genuinely so grateful that I have you in my life. After everything we've been through together, it still amazes me that somehow, through all the good days, bad days, stupid fights, misunderstandings, laughter, and countless conversations, we're still here.

Almost five years with you is a lot of memories to fit into one letter. We've grown together, changed together, annoyed each other countless times 😂, and still somehow found our way back to each other every time.

You have seen so many versions of me. The happy me, the dramatic me, the overthinking me, the annoying me, the emotional me, and probably the version of me that even I don't understand sometimes. And despite all of that, you continue to be there.

I know I'm not always the easiest person to love. Sometimes I overthink things, sometimes I get angry over the smallest things, sometimes I don't express what I'm actually feeling properly. I know I can hurt you, even when I don't mean to. And I'm sorry for those moments.

But beneath all of that, there is one thing I'm always sure about — you matter to me so much.

You have become such a big part of my life that sometimes I can't even imagine what my everyday life would look like without you. You're the person I want to tell random things to, the person I want to call when something happens, the person whose messages can change my entire mood.

And I love the little things about us too. Our random conversations. Our stupid jokes. The way we can talk about absolutely nothing and somehow spend so much time doing it. The comfort of knowing there's someone on the other side of the phone who knows me so well.

I don't know exactly what the future will look like. We still have so much to figure out, so much to achieve, and so many things ahead of us. But I hope that when we look back someday, we'll remember these years and smile at how young, stupid, emotional, and completely in love we were.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for choosing me even on the days when I'm difficult to understand. Thank you for loving me in your own way. And thank you for being you.

I hope you know that you aren't just my boyfriend. You're my comfort person, my favourite notification, my biggest headache 😂, my safe place, and one of the most important people in my life.

And if I don't say it enough—

I love you. A lot. More than I sometimes know how to express. ❤️

If I could give you one thing in life, I would give you the ability to see yourself through my eyes.

Maybe then you would understand just how special you are to me.

Thank you for being a part of my life, for all the memories we've made, for every laugh, every conversation, every little moment and everything in between.

I don't know what the future holds, but I know that I want to keep making memories with you.

Through the good days, the bad days, the silly days and all the ordinary days, I hope you always know that you are loved.

You are one of the most precious parts of my life.

And if I had to choose all over again, I'd still choose you.

Happy Boyfriend's Day to my person. Here's to us, to everything we've already survived, and to all the memories we haven't made yet.

Always yours, ❤️
